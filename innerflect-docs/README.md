## The Strategy in Brief

### Brand Foundation
**Innerflect = Inner Reflection**

Technology that enables inner reflection—for individuals, organizations, and systems. Looking within to understand what truly matters, then reflecting that understanding outward through systems that serve regeneration, not extraction.

**Tagline:** One Dashboard → Every Empty Spot Becomes Revenue

Or alternatively: **Systems with Soul**

### Business Model
**Regeneration Through Efficiency**

- **OPERATIONS** → Intelligent systems fill empty spots automatically (SpotPulse)
- **REGENERATION** → Better utilization = less waste, less acquisition cost, more efficiency
- **REVENUE** → Filled spots generate €9k-€86k/mo extra with same fixed costs

**The Cycle:** Revenue funds operations → Operations reduce waste → Waste reduction enables regeneration → Regeneration differentiates venue → Differentiation commands premium pricing → Premium pricing creates more revenue

### The PRIMARY Product: SpotPulse

**SpotPulse — Universal Empty-Spot-to-Revenue Engine** ⭐

**Works for:**
1. **Wellness/Fitness** (yoga studios, spas, retreat centers) — €14k-€58k/mo
2. **Hospitality** (hostels, boutique hotels, eco-resorts) — €2.7k-€13k/mo
3. **F&B/Events** (restaurants, beach clubs, event venues) — €12k-€58k/mo
4. **Catch-All** (coworking, photo studios, coaching) — €8k-€36k/mo

**Integration:** 45-90min setup, pre-built for 87% of market (Mindbody, Cloudbeds, Resy, Google Calendar, Booking.com)

**ROI:** 4-21 days proven across all verticals

**Pricing:** €1,490-€3,900/mo (flat, no rev-share)

**Scale Path:** €50M+ ARR by 2028 with 4-market expansion (Bali → Tulum → Byron Bay → Ibiza → Costa Rica)

### Future Products (Built After SpotPulse)

**SpotPulse Impact OS** — Impact tracking upsell (+€500-€2k/mo)

**SpotPulse RegenOS** — Full operations for premium properties (€8k-€25k/mo)

**SpotPulse Booking Engine** — Direct bookings with transaction fees (8-15% + €2k-€9k/mo base)

### Why SpotPulse Is The Main Application

1. **Universal:** Same platform works across 4+ industries
2. **Immediate ROI:** 4-21 days, not months
3. **Pre-integrated:** 87% of market ready out of the box
4. **High margin:** 85%+ gross margin
5. **Scalable sales:** One pitch deck adapts to all verticals
6. **Network effects:** Partner sharing creates viral loop
7. **Gateway product:** Natural path to Impact OS, RegenOS, Booking Engine
8. **Values-aligned:** Efficiency = waste reduction = regeneration
9. **Proven:** 34-72% fill rates in 2025 Bali pilots
10. **Fundable:** Clear path to €50M+ ARR
