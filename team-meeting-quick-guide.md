# Team Meeting Quick Guide

## 🎯 Meeting Structure

**Two 35-minute sessions with a 10-minute break**

---

## 📋 Session 1: Finishing Details (35 min)
*Focus: Working style, focus areas, availability, main strengths*

### 1. Main Strengths (8-9 min)
**Key Points:**
- Technical skills & tools
- What you bring to a team
- Best work types
- When you're most productive

### 2. Focus Areas & Interests (8-9 min)
**Key Points:**
- Areas to focus on/improve
- SpotPulse/Innerflect interests
- Problems you enjoy solving
- What you want to learn

### 3. Working Style & Preferences (8-9 min)
**Key Points:**
- Solo vs. collaboration preference
- What helps you do your best work
- Feedback style
- Communication style
- Support needs

### 4. Availability & Logistics (8-9 min)
**Key Points:**
- Hours/timezone/working hours
- Best way to reach you
- Concerns about working together
- Blockers & what makes work easier

---

## ☕ Break (10 min)
*Take notes, reflect, prepare for session 2*

---

## 🚀 Session 2: Solution & Approach (35 min)
*Focus: What we decide together, how to go about it, how to manage ourselves through the week*

### 5. Role & Contribution (8-9 min)
**Key Points:**
- Role you see yourself taking
- Coding-intensive vs. leading/coordinating
- Client-facing comfort
- Ideal coding/other balance

### 6. SpotPulse-Specific & Role Options (8-9 min)
**Key Points:**
- What excites you about SpotPulse
- Which role option resonates? (1-5)
- Experience with: APIs, dashboards, data viz, real-time, AI

### 7. Decision-Making & Collaboration (8-9 min)
**Key Points:**
- How you prefer to make decisions
- Approach to new problems/features
- Technical decision approach
- Handling disagreements
- What makes collaboration work

### 8. Week Management & Workflow (8-9 min)
**Key Points:**
- How to plan & track work
- Communication rhythm
- Sharing progress & blockers
- Staying aligned
- Handling priority/scope changes
- Open questions

---

## ✅ Checklist for Each Session

**Before Starting:**
- [ ] Review the 4 questions for this session
- [ ] Have note-taking ready
- [ ] Set timer for 35 minutes

**During:**
- [ ] Keep it conversational (not a checklist)
- [ ] Let people expand on interesting points
- [ ] Take notes on key insights
- [ ] Stay flexible - skip irrelevant parts

**After:**
- [ ] Review notes during break
- [ ] Identify action items
- [ ] Prepare for next session

---

---

## 💡 Key Reminders

- **Conversational, not checklist** - Let the conversation flow naturally
- **Take notes** - Capture strengths, preferences, concerns
- **Be flexible** - Adapt to what's relevant
- **End with action items** - What happens next? When do we start?
