Team Getting-to-Know Questions

Two 35-minute sessions with a 10-minute break

1 Session 1: Finishing Details 35 min

Focus: Working style, focus areas, availability, main strengths

1 Main Strengths 8-9 min

What are your strongest technical skills and what do you bring to a team?

  • Technical skills: frontend, backend, design, data analysis, etc.
  • Technologies/tools you feel most confident with
  • Something technical you've built or worked on that you're proud of
  • What you bring to a team: problem-solving, creativity, organization, communication
  • What type of work do you do best? (building from scratch, debugging, optimizing, designing)
  • When do you feel most productive/creative?

2 Focus Areas & Interests 8-9 min

What areas do you want to focus on or get better at, and what part of SpotPulse/Innerflect interests you most?

  • Areas you want to focus on or improve
  • What part of SpotPulse/Innerflect interests you most? (e.g., dashboard UI, backend logic, integrations, AI/automation)
  • What kind of problems do you enjoy solving?
  • What would you like to learn or explore more?

3 Working Style & Preferences 8-9 min

How do you prefer to work, and what helps you do your best work?

  • Work preference: solo deep work vs. collaboration, pair programming, async communication, regular syncs
  • What helps you do your best work? (clear requirements, autonomy, collaboration, feedback)
  • How do you like to receive feedback? (direct vs. written, in meetings vs. async)
  • Communication style: frequent updates, check-ins, async messages, detailed docs
  • What areas do you find challenging or want to improve?
  • What kind of support do you need to do your best work?

4 Availability & Logistics 8-9 min

What's your availability and how do we best work together?

  • Availability: hours per day/week, timezone, preferred working hours
  • What's the best way to reach you? (Slack, email, etc.)
  • Any concerns or things we should know about working together?
  • What slows you down or frustrates you in your work?
  • What would make your work life easier?

☕ Break

10 minutes — Take notes, reflect, prepare for session 2

2 Session 2: Solution & Approach 35 min

Focus: What we decide together, how to go about it, how to manage ourselves through the week

5 Role & Contribution 8-9 min

What role do you see yourself taking, and what's your preference?

  • What role do you see yourself taking? (lead developer, specialist, generalist)
  • Preference:
    • More coding-intensive work
    • Leading/coordinating (less coding, more planning/client-facing)
    • Mix of both
  • Are you comfortable with client-facing work? (meetings, demos, feedback sessions)
  • What's your ideal balance between coding and other responsibilities?

6 SpotPulse-Specific & Role Options 8-9 min

Looking at SpotPulse, what excites you most, and which tech role option resonates with you?

  • What part of SpotPulse excites you most?
  • Which tech role option resonates with you?
    • Option 1: By System Layer (Frontend/Backend/Intelligence)
    • Option 2: By Product Flow (Detection/Platform/Intelligence)
    • Option 3: By Technical Domain (Backend/Integration, Frontend/Client Relations, Intelligence)
    • Option 4: By Responsibility Scope (Integration Lead, Platform Developer, Intelligence)
    • Option 5: By Feature Area (Core Platform, Integrations, Intelligence)
  • What's your experience with:
    • API integrations (Mindbody, Cloudbeds, Resy, etc.)
    • Dashboard/UI development
    • Data visualization (charts, heat maps)
    • Real-time systems
    • Automation/AI

7 Decision-Making & Collaboration 8-9 min

How do we decide together and approach the work?

  • How do you prefer to make decisions? (consensus, lead decides, discuss then decide)
  • How do you like to approach new problems or features? (research first, dive in, discuss approach)
  • What's your approach to technical decisions? (documentation, proof of concept, team discussion)
  • How do you handle disagreements or different opinions?
  • What makes collaboration work well for you?

8 Week Management & Workflow 8-9 min

How do we manage ourselves through the week?

  • How do you prefer to plan and track work? (daily standups, weekly planning, task boards, async updates)
  • What's your ideal communication rhythm? (daily check-ins, weekly syncs, as-needed)
  • How do you like to share progress and blockers?
  • What helps you stay aligned with the team?
  • How do you prefer to handle changes in priorities or scope?
  • What questions do you have about the project or team?
  • Is there anything else you'd like us to know about you, your work style, or what you're looking for?

💡 Tips for the Meeting

Keep it conversational

Don't go through questions like a checklist

Let people expand

If someone shares something interesting, follow up

Take notes

Capture key points about strengths, preferences, concerns

Be flexible

Skip questions that don't apply or aren't relevant

End with action items

What happens next? When do we start?

Use the break

Take notes, reflect, prepare for session 2
